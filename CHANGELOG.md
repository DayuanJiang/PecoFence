# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The Peek search box no longer covers the icons it has just highlighted. It always opened in the upper middle of the screen, which on screens up to 1664 px wide (at 100 %) is over the left column of the first-run fences. It now opens beside a fence that would be under it (16 px away; below or above when there is no room beside), and a box that was opened empty moves once, when the first matches come in, if it covers a fence that holds them. It never moves while you type on, and with no clear place on the screen it stays in the upper middle.
- The Peek search list scrolls. With more than nine matches it showed the first nine: Down on the ninth row went back to the first, the wheel did nothing, and the other matches could only be opened by clicking them in their fences. Now Down on the last row shown, PageDown / PageUp and the wheel (three rows a notch) go through all of them, End jumps to the last row and Home back to the first, a thin thumb at the right edge shows where you are, and Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 open the rows on show. (Home still moves the caret while the highlight is on the first row, and End moves it unless it already is at the end of the text; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End always go to the list.) With 「速览搜索也找整台电脑」 on, every match in the fences is listed before Everything's results (five rows were kept for them), and the two action rows stay under the list.
- Peek search with a Chinese or Japanese input method: the first key opens the search box and the word is composed in it, at the caret. Before, with Microsoft Pinyin in Chinese mode the letters and their candidates showed in a small window at the top-left corner of the screen, and the box opened only once a candidate was taken (pressing Ctrl twice, which opens the box first, already worked). Typing in English mode and the other keys of a Peek (arrows, Enter, Delete, Esc) are unchanged.
- Names and titles in Fluent fences read on any wallpaper. With 「文字颜色」 on 「自动」, a Fluent fence took its text colour from the colour mode alone, while its see-through plate is as bright as the wallpaper behind it: in dark mode over a light wallpaper the names were white on a light plate (contrast 1.4 : 1, over the Windows 11 default wallpaper 2.0 to 2.3 : 1), in light mode over a dark wallpaper dark on dark (3.3 : 1). Each fence now picks dark or light text from what is behind it, as Liquid Glass fences do (13 : 1, 8.3 to 9.4 : 1 and 5.6 to 6.1 : 1 in the same three cases), and the hover, pressed, inactive selection, focus, scrollbar and active-tab colours switch with the text so they still show. A fence whose plate is partly too bright or too dark for its text (a busy wallpaper) gets the soft halo behind its names. Where the colour mode's own text already reads, nothing changes; a fence dragged from a light to a dark part of the wallpaper switches once; 「白色」 and 「黑色」 stay fixed, and the loose icons of a 「桌面」 fence arranged like the desktop keep their white names. During a Peek a light-mode fence shows its dark text, because it then floats over windows and not over the wallpaper. A theme file's `light` and `dark` text colours are used the same way.
- Ctrl+Z in a fence undoes the newest thing you did there, file operations too. Before, it only knew fence moves and resizes, icons moved between fences, a deleted fence and 「立即应用」: after a rename, a Delete or a drop into a folder it skipped that and undid an older fence or icon move instead. A rename made in a fence is now renamed back, and a folder or text file made from a fence goes to the Recycle Bin; a Delete, files dropped into a folder or onto the desktop and pasted copies are handed to Windows' own undo, as Ctrl+Z on the desktop does (if another program's file operation came after yours, Windows undoes that one first). A cut icon pasted into another fence is one step too. After a Shift+Delete, or a Delete that never reached the Recycle Bin (a USB stick, a network folder), Ctrl+Z does nothing and says 「永久删除的文件无法撤销。」 instead of undoing an older step. A shortcut made by an Alt-drag into a fence and a link made from a dropped address are steps too, and so is a file move started by `pecofence-cli`. Ctrl+Z pressed right after Delete waits for the delete to finish and then undoes it. Renaming a fence itself is still not a step.
- A copy pasted into a fence stays in that fence and is selected. Ctrl+C then Ctrl+V in a fence (or 「粘贴」 in its menu) made the 「xxx - Copy」 file and its icon appeared where the sorting rules sent it, mostly in 「桌面」, with nothing selected. The copies now land in the fence you pasted into, whatever the rules say, and everything a paste brings in (copies, cut icons, files from another folder) is selected and scrolled into view, as in File Explorer.
- An icon dragged in from another fence lands where you let it go. In a fence with the 「手动」 order, an icon from another fence showed no insertion line and went to the end, also when dropped on the middle of an icon that is no folder; only a reorder inside one fence had the line. The same line now shows and the icon lands at that place, in the icons, list and details views, and Ctrl+Z puts it back. An icon let go on empty space, on the title or on a tab still goes after the others, as do files that arrive by a rule.
- A dropped icon arrives from the pointer. At the release the drag picture vanished; a reordered icon then slid 250 ms from its old place across the others, and one dropped into another fence faded in at its cell, up to a few hundred pixels from the pointer. Dropped icons now start where their picture was and glide to their cells in 167 ms; the other icons slide as before.
- A drag of one to four icons out of a fence says where they go: 「移动到 <栅栏 / 文件夹 / 标签页的名字>」 beside the badge, as a drag out of File Explorer does (it showed the arrow alone; five or more icons already had the text). A reorder inside one fence keeps the badge alone.
- The rename box of an icon wraps like its name. It was one line 24 DIPs high and at least 96 DIPs wide in a 14 DIP font (names are 12): wider than the icon's cell, with the start of a long name scrolled out and the second line of the old name showing under it. In the icons view it is now as wide as the name, centred under the icon, in the names' own font and size, wraps as the name does, grows and shrinks while you type (up to 6 lines) and shows the name from its start; the old name under it is not drawn. List and details rows keep one line, in the row's font size.
- Renaming a folder, shortcut, program or picture keeps its icon on screen; the icon went blank for about a quarter of a second and faded back in. With Windows' animation effects off, a folder made in a fence (Ctrl+Shift+N or 「新建」) showed an empty tile until its name box closed; it now shows its icon at once.
- A locked fence says so: a lock sits before the roll-up arrow while the pointer is over the fence, with the tip 「已锁定位置和大小，右键可解除锁定」, and it blinks once when you try to drag the title or pull an edge outward. Before, a locked fence looked like any other and a drag did nothing without a sign. (A fence collapsed to its left tab column, or locked at its narrowest width, has no room for it.)
- Icons glide to their new places when dragging a fence's left or right edge changes the number of columns (167 ms); they jumped there in one frame, up to a few hundred pixels, while a sort or a new file already made them slide.
- Changing the view (图标 / 列表 / 详细信息), typing the first letter into a folder fence's filter box and clearing the filter cross-fade in 83 ms, like a tab switch without its slide, instead of cutting.
- The wheel scrolls at an even speed while it keeps turning. Every notch started the slow-down curve again, so the speed swung by about a third roughly ten times a second (19 to 55 pixels per frame over 8 notches, now 29 to 32). A notch that arrives while the content still moves the same way now carries its speed on; a single notch, a notch the other way, the keys and the scrollbar scroll as before.
- A fence let go a little over another one glides to the free spot beside it (167 ms) instead of jumping there, in both styles.
- Rolling a tall fence up no longer looks like a cut. The 167 ms roll-up used WinUI Expander's collapse curve, which does almost all of the way in the middle: a 560 px fence lost up to 288 px in a single frame. It now eases in and out over the same 167 ms (88 px a frame at most), as the Liquid Glass style always did; opening is unchanged.
- Peek: a fence that another program's window hides completely fades in with the darkened background and fades out with it (83 ms) instead of appearing and vanishing in one frame. Fences on the visible desktop do not change.
- When a Peek search ends, a fence the search scrolled to a match goes back to where it was, unless you scrolled it yourself during the search.
- The name box of a new fence appears once the fence has faded in (about 0.3 s after the click); it used to show at full strength on a fence that was still almost transparent. No box opens when the fence was hidden or covered by another window while it faded in. The rectangle you draw on the desktop stays, fainter, for as long as the 「新建栅栏」 and 「在此显示文件夹…」 buttons are offered; it used to vanish at the release, leaving the buttons under an empty area.
- After Delete the next icon is selected and takes the keyboard focus (the one before it at the end of the list), as in File Explorer, so Delete, Delete, Delete walks down a fence, also with 「删除」 from the item menu. Before, nothing was selected and the next arrow key jumped to the first icon.
- F2 with several icons selected renames the focused one (it did nothing). The first Esc in a filtered folder or smart fence clears the filter and keeps the selection, the next Esc clears the selection (one press cleared both). After a rename that moves the icon out of view in a sorted fence, the fence scrolls to it.
- The keyboard stays with the fences: after Enter or Esc in the name box of a fence, that fence takes the next keys (Ctrl+V into the fence just made works), and after 「删除栅栏」 from the menu the nearest fence in sight takes them, so the Ctrl+Z the notice suggests works at once. Before, the keyboard went to the taskbar or the desktop.
- An icon's name takes a wider box when that shows more of it: 「README.md」 no longer breaks into 「README.m」 and 「d」, six Chinese characters stay on one line and twelve show whole on two. A name gets 74 instead of 64 DIPs (under a 48 px icon) when the whole name fits there; longer names, and every name in a fence that scrolls, keep the usual room. Cells and fences keep their sizes.
- The text inside a fence starts at one left edge, the title's: the lines of notes, clocks and script fences, the 「名称」 column, the date group captions and the icons of list and details rows now start 16 DIPs from the edge (they were at 14, 12, 8 and 8).
- In a fence that scrolls, the strip along the right edge stays clear for the scrollbar: the size column of the details view ended 1 px before the thumb and the last icon column's selection ran under it. Columns now end 12 DIPs and hover and selection fills 8 DIPs before the edge.
- A title set to 「跟随色调」 reads on its own tinted title bar: it was the tint on the tint (contrast 1.1 : 1 in dark mode, 1.4 : 1 in light mode) and now moves lighter or darker, keeping its hue, until it reaches 3 : 1.
- A fence's corner resizes both ways along 16 px of each edge, as a normal window's does (it answered only in a 6 px square). A fence can no longer be made so narrow that its title is only 「…」: the narrowest width keeps the first two characters.
- While an edge is dragged, the shadow keeps up with the fence; it was redrawn about half as often as the window and trailed by up to 12 px.
- On a 60 Hz display the wheel glide, icons that slide to a new place and fades move in even steps again. Since 0.3.2 they drew 1.1 to 1.5 frames per refresh there, at uneven times: a wheel glide moved 80, 26, 53, 56, 88, 28 pixels in six refreshes where about 55 each is even. The timer that 0.3.2 added for PCs without a graphics card ended the wait for the display after 16 ms, just before a 60 Hz display's own tick (one every 16.67 ms). It is now set from the display's refresh rate, 2 ms after the moment the next tick is due, so a display that ticks always starts the frame itself; on PCs without a graphics card a missing tick still starts its frame on time. Displays of 120 and 144 Hz were not affected.
- When a fence rolls up, the fences that follow it move in the same frame and its shadow shrinks with it. Each step of the roll resized the fence first and moved its shadow and the fences below about 6 ms later (up to 20 ms), so on a PC with a graphics card a frame now and then (2 of 55 in a test) showed a bare strip of wallpaper between the shortened fence and the one below, or the old shadow outline standing on the wallpaper. They now take their places in one step, and the shadow shrinks just before the fence. Opening is unchanged: the fence grows and then pushes the others out from under it. (On PCs without a graphics card Windows shows a fence's new edge one frame before it shows moved windows; that is unchanged.)
- `pecofence-cli fence fit` glides the bottom edge to the new height like 「适应内容大小」 in the fence menu; it used to jump.
- A rolled-up fence leaves its hidden icons alone. A file selected before the fence rolled up stayed selected, and Delete on the rolled-up fence sent it to the Recycle Bin although nobody could see it (Enter, F2, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+A and the arrows acted on hidden icons the same way). These keys now do nothing on a rolled-up fence, the Menu key opens the fence menu, and the selection is dropped when the fence has rolled up; Tab, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+F work as before.
- A fence rolled up by a click on its arrow stays rolled up. With 「鼠标停在上面时」 expanding, it closed and opened again by itself 0.44 s later because the pointer still rested on the title; it now waits until the pointer has left the fence once. The same for a double-click on the title, the fence menu and the command line.
- An open fence no longer covers the fences it pushed down to the taskbar (since 0.3.1; 168 px of overlap in the test layout). A fence opened by a click or the command line now opens only as far as the fences below leave room, never under its title row plus 40 px, and its other icons scroll; its saved size stays, so it opens in full again as soon as there is room. A fence opened on hover still opens in full above them, as before.
- After icons slide (a file deleted, a sort, a reorder), the hover highlight is on the icon under the resting pointer; it stayed on the icon that had moved away until the pointer moved.
- Animations in a fence with thousands of files take less work: every frame went through all icons, shown or not. With 6000 files the slowest frame of a hover went from 0.8 to 0.4 ms and of a wheel scroll from 0.7 to 0.5 ms.
- Fences keep the same gap whether one was moved or resized next to the other: with a `snapping.gapPx` that does not give whole pixels at the display scale (5 at 150 %, 2 at 125 %) the two differed by 1 px. A tab's hover fade stays with its tab when the tabs are reordered.
- The fences keep answering while a large file is copied. Pasting a file of several GB onto the desktop stopped every fence for most of a minute (40 seconds for 6 GB on a test PC): while Windows writes such a file out, every other write to that disk waits, and PecoFence wrote its configuration and its log from the same thread that draws the fences. Both are now written in the background. Quitting, signing out, importing a configuration and `pecofence-cli` commands still wait until the configuration is on the disk.
- Quitting PecoFence while the name box of a fence is open no longer ends with an error at exit.
- After 「显示桌面」 (Win+D), a window that shows without coming to the front no longer stays under the fences. The fences float over the raised desktop until another window comes to the front; a program's window that showed first and came to the front later stayed under the floating fences meanwhile. With a window that is still black, the screen looks like a wallpaper gone black with only the icons and the taskbar left, which is what #187 reports after opening a file with WPS Office (reproduced with a stand-in window; WPS itself was not tested). The fences now go under such a window within about a quarter of a second and float again if it closes while the desktop is still shown. Windows that are always on top do not count.
- The log says more about 「显示桌面」: which desktop windows Explorer has when it starts, ends and when the window that holds the icons changes (Windows 10 and Windows 11 up to 23H2 make and destroy them), how long it lasted and whether the window that ended it was maximized.
- A note, clock or script fence made on Settings › 小工具 appears beside the Settings window. A new note went to the middle of the screen, under the window: only its edit box showed over the settings page, and after a click elsewhere the note was out of sight until the window was moved or closed; a clock or a weather fence went to the top left, partly under the window. Each now takes the free place beside the window on the side with more room, level with its top, or the nearest free place the window leaves clear when that one is taken; where the window and the fences leave no such place, the one the window covers least. Tools made from the tray menu, a fence menu or the command line go where they did.
- Windows notifications of PecoFence name their sender 「PecoFence」. The line above each notification, and the entry in Settings › System › Notifications, showed the program file's description cut off, 「PecoFence: group your desktop icons into…」, in every language. The file description is now 「PecoFence」; Task Manager and the list of startup apps show it too. This is for the installed and the portable edition (the Store edition already showed its package name), and Windows takes the new name with the update.
- One kind of quotation marks per language. Simplified Chinese mixed 「 」 and “ ”, on one card too (「已删除栅栏“临时文件”」 above 「点「撤销」…」): the 21 texts with 「 」 now use “ ” like the other 62. Korean mixed ‘ ’, ' ' and “ ” (now “ ” in all 73), and two Italian tips had “ ” where the others have « ».
- 「下一条」 on the first-start tips changes the texts on the card that is there. Each tip was a card of its own: the old one vanished, one frame showed none, and the next faded in at another height, three blinks on the way through four tips. The tips now share one card, as tall as the tallest of them, so the title, the button and the × stay where they are.
- One gap between fences, the one set under 「栅栏间距」 in Settings (8 px unless changed). The first-run fences stood 16 px apart and 16 px from the screen edges, a fence made from another fence's menu 12 px from it, and only a dragged fence kept the 8 px. The first-run column, a fence made from a fence menu or the tray menu, and a rectangle drawn within 10 px of a fence now all keep that gap with the edges in line. A fence made from the tray menu while its spot is taken lands beside the fence there, no longer in the top-left corner of the screen.
- A tool made from a fence's menu (新建便签, 新建小工具) stands beside that fence, the snapping gap away with the tops in line; it landed touching the fence and 4 px higher. The script examples are titled as their menu lines in every language (「PC status」; it was the file's name, 「pc-status」).
- A smart fence that waits for Everything offers it on the spot: under the sentence in the empty fence there is a row, 「启动 Everything」 or 「安装 Everything」, that does what the same entry of its menu does. The fence used to say only that a right-click can start or install it.
- A note is edited in place and keeps its look. A click on a note put a white box over it (grey in dark mode): square corners over the fence's rounded ones, a scroll bar that was always there, another font for Chinese on a non-Chinese Windows, and every line moved (about 5 px to the left, 19 px apart in place of 25, check boxes turned into 「[ ]」, a long line wrapped at another place). Now nothing changes but a blinking caret: the same font, size, lines, check boxes, colours and background, in both styles and both colour modes. The caret starts where you clicked (after the last line in a new note and with 「编辑便签」), a line typed as 「[ ] …」 shows its check box at once, Backspace right after a check box removes it, Up and Down follow the lines as the note wraps them, a drag selects, a double-click takes a word, and a right-click in the text still offers cut, copy, paste and select all.
- Text that does not fit in a note or a script fence scrolls. It was cut in the middle of a line and the wheel did nothing; the only way to see the rest was to make the fence bigger. The wheel now scrolls it like a list (Windows' lines per notch, the same glide; at once with animations off), the thin scrollbar shows while it does and can be dragged, and the text comes to rest with a whole line at the top edge. A script fence's footer stays in place. A note that is being edited scrolls to keep the caret in view.
- A script that prints something and then fails shows what went wrong. The fence showed only the first line the script had printed, in the normal colour, and the error message nowhere; only the footer said 「运行出错（退出代码 1）」. The line that says what went wrong now comes first, in red (the first line of the error text, or the last line of a Python traceback), then everything the script printed until it failed; the footer still gives the exit code.
- A click on a clock or on a script fence's text also saves and closes an open note editor; these fences take no focus, so the editor stayed open. After Esc or Ctrl+Enter the keyboard is with the note's fence; it went to the taskbar.
- A narrow clock shows a shorter date in place of a cut one: 「11月12日 周四」 (「Thu, Nov 12」) when the long date 「2026年11月12日 星期四」 does not fit on one line at the smallest date size, and no date when that does not fit either. It showed the start of the long date and a clipped second line.
- 「编辑脚本」 and 「编辑便签」 in the fence menu have a pencil icon; they had the same icon as 「重命名…」.
- Peek search: the result row and the type tab under the pointer get a hover fill (83 ms fade, as an icon in a fence), and a row shows the hand cursor. They did not react to the pointer at all, although a click opens the row as Enter does. The highlighted row stays where the keys put it.
- Peek search: a file that two fences show (a folder fence and a smart fence over the same folder) is listed once, with 「<栅栏> 等 2 个栅栏」 under its name, and counted once in 「N 个结果」. It took one row per fence, so three of nine rows could be repeats. Of equally good matches the list keeps the one in the leftmost fence (then the topmost); the fences still highlight every copy.
- Peek search: the empty search box says what can be typed: 「搜索栅栏里的文件，可以用拼音」, or 「搜索栅栏和整台电脑，可以用拼音」 when the whole-computer search is on and Everything is running (other languages leave out the pinyin part), in the secondary text colour in light and dark mode. It was an empty strip.
- Peek search: the search box follows the Windows accent colour (matched letters, the highlighted row, the chosen type tab, the glyphs of the action rows), also when the colour changes while the box is open. It was always blue, next to fences that mark the same matches in the accent colour.
- Peek search: the bottom bar writes 「Enter 打开」. The Enter key was a 「↵」 about 6 px high in a fallback font, much smaller than the text beside it.
- Peek search: a folder path too long for its row is cut in the middle: the drive and the last folders stay (「C:\…\客户往来文件\最终版 A」), where the end was cut and results from neighbouring folders read the same. The path line uses the row's full width, and the pointer resting on a whole-computer result shows the full path in a tip.
- Peek search: the result list is drawn off screen and shown in one step. On the first search after signing in to Windows, while Windows was still loading the fonts, the list window stood empty and its rows came in one after another over about a tenth of a second.
- Peek search: the typed text starts at the same left edge as the result names and the magnifier sits over the row icons (the text was 13 px further right, the magnifier 2 px).
- Peek search: Chinese typed in the search box is drawn in the same font as the list (Microsoft YaHei UI and its counterparts) on a Windows in another language too, where it came out in a lighter fallback font.
- Peek search: the search box has a thin outline, so it keeps its edge over a white window when 「速览时调暗背景」 is off.
- Peek search: on the two action rows (「在 Everything 里查看全部结果」, 「把这次搜索放到桌面」) and on system items the bottom bar shows only 「Enter 打开」; it also offered 「Ctrl+Enter 打开所在文件夹」 and 「→ 更多操作」, which do nothing there.
- The smart fence editor (「新建智能栅栏…」, 「编辑条件…」) shows all of its rows in every language. Its window opened at 680 × 560: once a template filled the list, the list scrolled in all eleven interface languages (the second line of the type buttons was cut off in Chinese and English too); in German, Portuguese and Russian 「选择…」 dropped under its row; in those three, Spanish and Italian 「取消」 and 「创建」 dropped under the count; and the first menu of a row was 132 px wide in every language, which cut «Расположение» to «Расположени». The window now opens at 880 × 660 (as large as the work area on a smaller screen), the first menu of every row is as wide as its longest entry in the language in use, and each of the five templates, or four conditions of any kind, shows without a scrollbar and with every control on its own line. More rows than fit scroll as before.
- The rename box of an icon and the name box of a fence take the colours of the text they cover. Since fence text follows the wallpaper, a fence in dark mode over a light wallpaper has dark names on a light plate, but the box stayed the dark mode's: a dark block with white text in the middle of it (and a white block in light mode over a dark wallpaper). The box now has the name's own text colour on a light field for dark text and a dark field for light text, in both styles and with 「文字颜色」 set to 「白色」 or 「黑色」. Loose icons arranged like the desktop keep the colour mode's box, as on the Windows desktop.
- Liquid Glass: the hover highlight shows on a bright wallpaper. With dark text the hover fill was white, which changed a nearly white glass by 2 levels of brightness or less: the icon under the pointer looked like the others. The fill now darkens the glass, like the pressed one (19 levels on the brightest glass, 7 on the darkest glass that still has dark text). Fences with light text keep their white fill.
- The expanded scrollbar ends inside the fence's rounded corner. In Liquid Glass (16 DIP corners) its lower arrow was cut along the corner; the bar now stops 10 DIPs above the bottom edge there and 2 DIPs in the Fluent style, follows a theme file's corner radius, and stays clear of the top corner when the tabs are in a column on the left.
- A double-click on one of Windows' own desktop icons no longer hides the fences. With 「Windows 桌面图标」 shown beside the fences, PecoFence told a press on an icon from one on the bare desktop by the icon Explorer marks as under the pointer, and Explorer marks it only when the pointer moves over it. With the pointer already on an icon when the desktop came into view (after 「显示桌面」 with Win+D, or when the window over it closed), a double-click opened the file and hid every fence, as a double-click on the bare desktop does. The icon the first click selected now tells the two apart; a double-click on the bare desktop hides and shows the fences as before.
- Settings: an open drop-down list looks like the rest of the window. It was the browser's own list (a square box, 27 px rows, a light grey bar for the current choice, in dark mode too); it now has rounded corners, 36 px rows and a tint on the current choice, in light and dark mode. The keys are the same: on a closed list the arrow keys, Home, End and typing change the choice, Enter or Alt+Down opens it, Esc closes it. A WebView2 runtime older than 135 keeps the old list. The navigation, the search results, the feedback box and its preview scroll with the thin scrollbar of the page instead of the wide default one.
- 「查找设置」: the result that Enter opens has an accent tint; in light mode it was #fafafa on #fbfbfb (1.01 : 1) and could not be told from the other rows. Saved snapshots, daily backups and your rules are no longer listed as if they were settings (「快照」 listed every snapshot by name). Words that found nothing now find their setting: 帮助, 教程 (使用手册), 关于, 版本号, 命令行, CLI, 终端 (让 AI 助手整理桌面), 撤销, 恢复布局 (把当前布局存为快照), dark and light in a Chinese interface, and the text on a card's buttons, in all eleven languages.
- Settings by keyboard: the navigation is one Tab stop. Tab went through 「查找设置」 and all seven pages before it reached the page's first control, the arrow keys did nothing in the navigation, and after Enter on a page six more Tabs were needed. Now Up, Down, Home and End move between the pages, Enter or Space shows one, and the next Tab is in that page.

## 0.1.2

- Liquid Glass reworked after kube.io's refraction model: the face of a fence stays perfectly clear and all the bending sits in the rim, where a convex squircle bezel refracts the wallpaper with Snell's law (index 1.5). The very edge mirrors the backdrop like thick glass, and the magnification eases out over 40 DIP so no inner frame shows between the rim and the clear face. A thin specular ring is filled with the refracted wallpaper's colour, strongly saturated. The old material's whole-panel warp, blur and colour fringes are gone, and Liquid Glass corners are now 16 DIP. Moving a fence draws faster (one displacement pass instead of three), and GPU memory peaks lower while arranging fences (about 160 instead of 450 MB with 7 fences on a 4K monitor).
- Fences line up when resized, whatever their icon size, spacing or view: a dragged edge, top and bottom included, snaps to other fences' edges (level, or a gap apart) and to the work area's edges, with alignment guides, also with 「调整大小时保持为整数个图标」 on. Away from them that option still sizes to whole columns and rows; at a height of no whole rows the rows share the space left over like the columns share the width, so a fence still shows whole icons only. Dragged edges, expanding a rolled fence and the automatic size changes stop exactly a gap short of the next fence instead of a whole row short. Switching the option on, a late DPI change at logon, tab changes and restoring a layout no longer re-round sizes; changing a fence's icon size, spacing, view or label lines still does (#1).
- Tab names no longer vanish on a narrow fence: squeezed tab pills give up their padding (down to 8 DIP) before a caption is shortened with an ellipsis.
- Alignment guides while dragging (「显示对齐参考线」, `snapping.guideLines`, on for new configurations): thin white lines along the edges a moved or resized fence lines up with, and along the screen's centre line when it snapped there.
- Dragged fences also snap to the screen's centre lines (centred horizontally / vertically on the work area). Holding Alt while dragging or resizing moves freely, without snapping.
- When a dragged fence's title is over another fence's title, that fence gets the white outline: releasing there adds the dragged fence to it as a tab. The outline used to disappear in that spot, which looked like a glitch.
- The drop outline no longer stays on screen after a Liquid Glass drag is cancelled with Esc or a right-click, and no longer flashes on top of the dragged fence every two seconds (the desktop-layer check took it for a foreign window).
- The 「在此新建栅栏」 menu that appears after drawing a rectangle on the empty desktop closes again when you click elsewhere or press Esc. It used to stay open until its item was chosen, and rectangles drawn meanwhile queued up more of them.
- The settings window follows Windows 11 Settings: neutral colours, plain 20 DIP icons, 14 / 12 px text and On / Off captions beside every switch; the slogans, the banner picture and the tinted sidebar are gone. Fluent and Liquid Glass are picked from two preview tiles that show the icon size, tint and Chameleon settings live. Icon tint and fence tint are colour swatches; alignment guides and the Peek hotkey sit under the switch they depend on and turn grey while it is off. Rules are one compact row each, the quick-add templates are chips inside their card, and the new-rule form opens on demand. The fence picker heads the 「栅栏」 page, and restoring the desktop icons moved to 「故障排除」 on the About page. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text in the window uses the font of the interface language, and 「显示语言」 is listed under 「外观」 again.
- Chinese file names and titles in fences are drawn with Microsoft YaHei UI. They used to come out in the Japanese UI font, with Japanese forms of shared characters (今, 令, 骨, 直) next to YaHei for simplified-only ones. Like Explorer, fence text follows the Windows display language: Traditional Chinese uses Microsoft JhengHei UI, Japanese Yu Gothic UI, Korean Malgun Gothic, every other language YaHei.
- Select several fences by drawing a rectangle on the empty desktop: every fence it touches is selected as you draw (Ctrl or Shift adds to the selection) and gets a white outline, like the other drag feedback. Drag any selected fence by its title and the whole group moves along, keeping its layout; the group's outline snaps to the other fences and the screen edges, Esc or a right-click puts it back. A click on the desktop or on any fence ends the selection. While the desktop icons are hidden PecoFence draws the selection rectangle itself, in white too (Explorer shows none then). A rectangle that touches no fence still offers 「在此新建栅栏」, and a drag that activates the desktop from another window now counts too.
- 「鼠标悬停时才显示标题栏」 no longer leaves an empty band at the top of every fence: at rest the title row folds away and the glass starts at the icons; when the pointer arrives, the glass grows back up and the title fades in, with the icons staying where they are.
