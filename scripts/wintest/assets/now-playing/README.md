# Now playing

Shows the song or video playing now (what Windows shows in its media flyout): the title, the
artist, the album, the cover and where it is, with buttons for the previous item, play or pause
and the next one. A line says when Spotify runs.

Permissions: `media: "control"` (sees what plays, may pause and skip, only when you click a
button) and `apps: ["spotify.exe"]` (knows whether Spotify runs).
