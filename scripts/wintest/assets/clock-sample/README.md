# Clock 时钟

The time in large type and the date under it, in your Windows region format (12 or 24 hours, the
long date with the weekday), as PecoFence's own clock shows them. The text grows and shrinks with
the fence; in a narrow fence the date takes its short form.

Turn on *Show seconds* in the fence menu, Plugin settings, to update the clock every second;
otherwise it updates every minute. It updates just after the clock turns, and at once when you
change the system time, the time zone or the region format. While the fence is rolled up or
hidden it does nothing.

Sends nothing over the network and asks for no permissions.

---

大字显示时间，下面是日期，按 Windows 的区域格式（12 或 24 小时制，长日期带星期），和 PecoFence 自带的时钟一样。
字的大小跟着栅栏变，栅栏窄了日期换成短的写法。在栅栏菜单的「插件设置」里打开「显示秒」后每秒更新一次，
关着时每分钟更新一次。改了系统时间、时区或区域格式时马上更新；栅栏收起或隐藏时不运行。
不联网，不需要任何权限。
