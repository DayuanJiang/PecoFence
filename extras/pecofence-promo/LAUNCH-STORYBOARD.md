# PecoFence — 28-second launch film

The September 11 product refresh replaces the 75-second walkthrough as the primary
promo. English copy throughout. Native recordings must come from the current build.

| Time | Audience takeaway | Picture | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | A desktop worth coming back to | Close, moving reveal of the current glass groups | A calmer desktop. |
| 3–8 s | Beautiful groups bring order | Pull back to a complete, staged Liquid Glass desktop | Beautifully organized. |
| 8–15 s | Several projects fit in one place | Native Work / Art tab switch and separation | Work. Play. Switch. |
| 15–23 s | Files stay within reach during work | Current groups rise above a staged project window with Peek | One shortcut away. |
| 23–28 s | A clear product and an invitation | Clean brand finish, with the real product in view | PecoFence / Make room for focus. |

Creative rules:

- One benefit per scene. No numbered steps, chapter progress bars, tutorial
  captions, settings tours or lists of secondary features.
- Feature proof takes most of the screen. Brief English headlines carry the story.
- Keep a restrained visual style: large type, deep glass color, deliberate camera
  motion, crisp cuts, and an original instrumental track.
- The live desktop uses staged folders/files. The other window is a staged project
  brief. Record only the staged demo desktop; never reuse outdated interface footage.
- Record Liquid Glass with `themeStyle: liquidGlass` and native `language: en`.
- Include free/open-source/Windows in the closing card, without performance claims.

The earlier 30-second brand film and 75-second walkthrough remain available.
